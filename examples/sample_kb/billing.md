# Billing

## Invoices

Invoices are issued on the first of each month and emailed to the billing
contact. Past invoices are available under Settings, then Billing History.

## Duplicate charges

If a card was charged twice for the same period, the duplicate is refunded
within five working days. Contact support with both invoice numbers and the
last four digits of the card.

## Changing plan

Upgrades take effect immediately and are charged pro rata for the remainder
of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next
billing period, so the current period keeps the features already paid for.

## Cancelling

Cancel under Settings, then Subscription. Access continues until the end of
the paid period. No partial refunds are issued for unused time.
